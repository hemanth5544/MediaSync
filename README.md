# 🚀 MediaSync(Beta)

Welcome to the **Beta Release** of **MediaSync** – a real-time communication platform featuring video & audio calling, chat, screen sharing, and streaming.

---

## 🔧 Version
**v0.1.0-beta**

---

## ✨ Features

- **🔊 Audio & 🎥 Video Calling**  
  Communicate in real-time with high-quality video and audio.

- **💬 Real-Time Chat**  
  Instant messaging support between users for quick and smooth interactions.

- **🖥️ Screen Sharing**  
  Share your screen with another user for effective collaboration.

- **📡 Streaming Support**  
  Stream your content reliably to others.
